# Support

Myrelith is an experimental prerelease maintained in spare time.

- For reproducible bugs, use the repository's bug-report issue form.
- For scoped ideas, use the feature-request form.
- For security or privacy matters, follow `SECURITY.md` or `PRIVACY.md` and do
  not publish sensitive information.

Please include your browser and operating-system versions, the exact steps,
what you expected, and what happened. Never upload private media; use a tiny
synthetic fixture when a file is needed to reproduce a problem.
