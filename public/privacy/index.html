Privacy

WebCut edits on your device. Your source videos, audio, images, and project files are not uploaded to WebCut or Cloudflare.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking pixels. No cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is disabled.

Who is responsible

WebCut is maintained by Aryel in Belgium. For privacy questions or requests, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile. Do not put private media or personal information in a public issue.

Data stored on your device

WebCut may use browser storage for:

  • preferences and the last export selection;
  • browser-managed file handles for recent projects and remembered media, when you grant access;
  • bounded crash-recovery snapshots containing project names, timeline structure, settings, and source metadata; and
  • temporary decoded media, previews, and export data in memory while you edit.

Recovery storage does not contain source media bytes. File handles are opaque browser permissions, not saved filesystem paths. Browser storage remains until you remove an item in WebCut, clear site data, or the browser evicts it. Closing a tab clears working memory.

Data processed by the host

Cloudflare Pages delivers and protects this site. It may process IP address, request time and URL, browser/device information, referrer, language or locale, and security events, and may produce aggregate traffic and country statistics. WebCut uses Cloudflare on the basis of the maintainer's legitimate interests in securely delivering and protecting the site. Cloudflare may process data outside the EEA using safeguards described in its terms, including EU Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Read the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Customer DPA.

Sharing and automated decisions

WebCut does not sell personal data or use it for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Request data is shared with Cloudflare only as needed to host and protect the site, or disclosed if legally required.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, and withdraw consent when consent is the legal basis. Most WebCut app data exists only in your browser and can be deleted by clearing the site's data. The maintainer cannot retrieve media or local data that was never sent.

Contact the maintainer for hosted-site requests. You may also complain to your local authority; in Belgium this is the APD/GBA.

Changes

This notice may change when the app, hosting, or data practices change. Material changes will be dated and published here.
